## §3 THE SEAM

Terrain tokens (tick_terrain_tokens:418-485) close a SHAPE-half-only momentum loop back into archetype priors; population never advances token memory, and the primary-vs-padding invariant — `ensure_tile` (tile_world.hpp:526-533) ticks tokens, `ensure_tile_padding` (tile_world.hpp:535-540) omits the tick — is itself a frozen draw-order fact (else every later primary tile's archetype priors diverge).
