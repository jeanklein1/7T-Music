# REBUILD-0 m3 — THE RECIPE (proposal; awaiting Jean's stamp)

m1 and m2 are landed and green. Deriving m3's cut from the censuses
surfaced one THEORY TENSION and two DESIGN FORKS the stamp language
does not resolve. Per the standing instruction (when in doubt, the
lens — or ask), this note asks before the mass conversion: m3 touches
every module signature and every call site, and a wrong interpretive
cut there is the most expensive mistake available in this campaign.

## 1 — THE TENSION (v3 §7 vs the stamped stage order)

The stamped order says: "m3 dissolution and pair merges in reach
order." Theory v3 §7 — ratified WITH the stamp, and the lens governs
where framing differs — re-frames exactly this work: "THE DISSOLUTION
TOOLBOX — A2 and A3 are NO LONGER AN ERA but tools, pulled per joint
when a demo axis demands substitution there; first named candidate:
the S2 boundary face (its four surfaces are already censused)."

Read strictly, v3 says: do NOT dissolve all seventeen modules in reach
order; pull the tool per joint, starting where a demo axis demands
substitution — the S2 boundary face (D1: the surface cast is the
declared second cast; its boundary must become substitutable first).

## 2 — THE MECHANICAL FORK (what the censuses prove either way)

**The reach-order plan self-blocks.** Converting fewest-deps modules
first does not free them of the keyhole while the SERVICES they call
still take Cartridge*: ribbon calls find_patch / run_spawn_preamble /
negotiate_position; even population_themes' one DIAG call takes c.
The services convert LAST in reach order — so every earlier convert
either keeps c (dissolution incomplete) or threads a Cartridge*
through its deps struct (dissolution fake). The only order that fully
frees a module is services-first — which inverts the stamped order
and converts the two heaviest seams (spawn_engine, entity_pipeline,
patch_system) before any body.

**The table seam.** FAMILY_DISPATCH rows (select/place/commit/evict/
prepare funnels) take Cartridge* by stamped seam (SEAM[spine:owns],
K2-related; the template keyhole is a stamped pattern law). If the
rows keep the keyhole — as stamped — then every family module keeps a
keyhole face regardless of its deps struct, permanently.

**Pair merges.** Unspecified anywhere: if it means hpp+inl merge into
one pre-class header once a module no longer needs the complete
Cartridge, that retires the zone-include architecture (fix-2,
self-wrapping law, zone census) for converted modules — a structural
migration larger than the deps structs themselves, and it can only
apply to modules with ZERO keyhole residue (per the table seam, that
may be none of the twelve families).

first PULL of the dissolution toolbox, not an era:
