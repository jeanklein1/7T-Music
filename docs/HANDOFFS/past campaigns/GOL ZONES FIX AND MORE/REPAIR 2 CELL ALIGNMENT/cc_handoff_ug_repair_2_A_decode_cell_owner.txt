WHY
---
ug_decode holds the owning cell in `cell` and then discards it:

    d.cellx = min(d.vx / UG_QUADS, PATCH_CELL_N - 1u);

For a cap vertex at lx = 4, d.vx = cell_x*4 + 4, so d.vx / 4 = cell_x + 1.
The cap's east column is assigned to the EAST neighbour, its north row to
the NORTH neighbour, its NE corner vertex to the DIAGONAL.

The rule is correct for the legacy grid: a legacy quad lies wholly inside
one cell and its min-corner vertex names that cell. It is wrong for the
cap band, where lx = 4 is the cell's own MAXIMUM edge, not the next cell's
minimum. One rule, two topologies.

Consequences, both reported from the rig:

  - The raised footprint is the checker square displaced one quad
    (PATCH_EXTENT / PATCH_MESH_N = 0.78125 wu = a quarter cell) toward
    -X and -Z. A lifted cell borrows a quarter-cell fringe of its WEST
    and SOUTH neighbours' colour and loses the same width of its own on
    EAST and NORTH.

  - A lifted cell's NE corner vertex takes the diagonal's lift, while the
    two dead cells between take that same lift along one edge each. The
    three strips form a continuous raised ribbon from one checker's
    corner to its diagonal's plateau: the observed membrane.

  - Render and physics disagree by that quarter cell:
    query_ground_walker_pair reads sample_live_card_gol at the pawn's own
    XZ, cell-exact. The pawn walks the true square; the rendered square
    is displaced.

GEOMETRY IS UNCHANGED BY THIS HANDOFF. d.vx / d.vz are not touched, so
vertex POSITIONS and the C++ index buffers are byte-identical. Only which
cell's lift each vertex takes changes. No C++ edit in this commit.
