the split are all verified. What is wrong is GEOMETRY: three influence
radii were authored in the wrong space, and none of our instruments has
any power over "is 8 wu the right radius." The felt result:
  · agents flee from 50 wu and can never be caught (or possessed);
  · cubes never react at all — their gate is UNSATISFIABLE;
  · spheres shove the player, which was never a goal.
The arithmetic, verified in-tree:
  1. point-source gate `ppr = personal_radius(30) + point_bubble_radius(20)`
     = 50 wu = one full PATCH_EXTENT. Inside it the escape is a FLOOR
     with gain 1.2 and NO proximity term ⇒ agents hold 18 wu/s away
     from a 15 wu/s player, from a patch away, forever. K2a's
     FLEE_SHELL_FRAC was scoped to the body-to-body gate and never
     touched this path.
  2. cube home = ground + orbit_height + bob ⇒ a cube floats ~15–25 wu
     ABOVE the pawn. The C1a gate is 3D: pd2 = pdx²+pdy²+pdz². With
     CUBE_PART_RADIUS = 8, pdy alone (≈20) gives pd2 ≥ 400 > 64 — the
     gate CANNOT FIRE at any lateral distance. The cube-vs-pawn contact
     radius (3.0 + 1.6 = 4.6) is deader still. C1a and K2b are each
     right alone and jointly fatal.
  3. `update_player_agent` carries the sphere loop with
     r = contact_radius(1.6) + CONTACT_SPHERE_RADIUS(12) = 13.6 wu and
     NO mass weighting — the player takes the full impulse. That is the
     T2b spec as written; K1 only made it visible.
