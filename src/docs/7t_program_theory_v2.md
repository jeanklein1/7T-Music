## 0 — THE INTENTION (carried)
An API understood immediately: logical, compositional, no foggy
reasoning. Fog is influence traveling undeclared paths; the theory
forbids it structurally and names everything else so that reading a
declaration is knowing the thing. The payoff is GENERATIVITY: NOVELTY
IS RECOMBINATION — new species are new points in trait-space over
existing machinery, not new subsystems.

## 1 — ROLES AND CASTS (carried)
Every noun is a ROLE (declared interface with laws) or a CAST (an
implementation currently playing it). COMPOSITION IS CASTING: a build
is a set of casts satisfying one another's requirements; customization
is a recast; the roster is the casting sheet generalized. Swapping a
cast may never require editing another cast — only the role's contract
binds them.

## 2 — THE LAYERS AND THE CENTRAL LAW (carried, one reconciliation)
L0 SUBSTRATE · L1 PRIMITIVES · L2 ENTITIES · L3 COUPLINGS · L4
ORCHESTRATION · L5 REALIZATION. The central law: EVERY BEHAVIOR IS
EITHER AUTONOMOUS OR COUPLED; THERE IS NO THIRD KIND. Coupled =
through a declared channel with a name, one author, a REST reproducing
the autonomous self exactly, a composition law, and the categorical
boundary (receivers get values, never sources).
RECONCILIATION (v2): COMPOSITION LAWS COME IN TWO KINDS. Scalar
deviations compose ADDITIVELY over inviolate idleness (the pipes'
law, held). Intent authorship composes by ARBITRATION — declared
priority among drivers; possession (K5) was always the precedent.
Rest = identity holds identically in both: all foreign authors
silent, the autonomous self remains, byte-for-byte. The generalized
silence test is unchanged. Primitive law (held): entities share
functions and vocabulary freely, NEVER state.

## 3 — THE SUBSTRATE (carried)
L0 is the stage all interaction is measured against — the one
MANDATORY coupling. 3a K1 HAS MODES: BOUND (surface owns position +
orientation), REFERENCED (own authority, consults the surface),

ground (stamps); the Surface interface carries the authoring channel.
3c THE LOCAL-FRAME LAW: entities speak frame_at — base + local normal
— never world axes; assuming Y-up or flat XZ is covert plane-coupling.
Geodesic corollary held: the swap price is exactly the local-frame
discipline.

## 4 — THE OCCUPIER ROLE AND THE COVERING LAW (carried)
OCCUPIER: a thing that claims a footprint and teaches the terrain to
cover it. THE COVERING LAW: the terrain covers what its occupiers
claim. 4a collision already exists unnamed — the occupier evaluators
ARE inside/edge/height queries; naming ONE query face over the
existing machinery IS the collision system; new shapes are casts under
the standing byte-pin + bounded-loop + uniform-dispatch laws. 4b the
honest boundary: a heightfield is one-height-per-column; substrate-
direction generalization is a declared FUTURE cast, named, not
pretended.

## 5 — THE ENTITY, COMPLETE (carried)
An entity is: CONTRACT (two faces: the OFFER — couplable surface,
datasheet, Placeable core, skin mount; and the REQUIREMENTS — what it
consumes and what it COSTS) + DESIGN (the seed is the whole biography)
+ BEHAVIOR (autonomous laws; owns runtime state; lifecycle its
temporal envelope; anatomy per §10) + COUPLINGS (declared channels).
THE ROSTER IS A DEPENDENCY RESOLVER over requirements faces. (v2 note:
under §8, a module's include list IS its requirements face.)

## 8 — THE ARROW LAW (v2; the compile-time doors law)
Every file dependency points from root toward leaf, never back. The
COMPOSITION ROOT may know everyone; no one may know the root. A
MODULE includes only contracts, services, and primitives; its include
list IS its requirements face. PEERS may know each other's
VOCABULARY, never each other's STATE. A DTO that exists to cross a
boundary belongs to the boundary's CONTRACT, not to either side (the
LADDER-5 refinement). MINIMALITY CLAUSE: a module exists per OWNED
DECISION, never per concept-that-sounds-separate (M-c precedent:
"floater" owned nothing and got no module).
THE OBSERVABLE — the deletion test: delete a module and the only file
that changes is the root (one include, one instance, one score line,
one roster bit). Anything else that breaks is a named violation.
Fog was influence on undeclared paths; entanglement is KNOWLEDGE on
undeclared paths — same disease, same cure.

## 9 — THE DRIVER LAW + THE FOUR STRATA (v2)
A moving thing is a BODY times a DRIVER.
THE BODY owns what survives any driver: K1 mode, constraints (floors,
clamps), the integrator (one control law), the GPU realization. THE
DRIVER is a cast of the authorship role; three author kinds: INPUT
(K3/K5), ALGORITHM (the autonomous self), MUSIC (the bank). All write
INTENTS through the body's integrator; arbitration (§2's second
composition kind) decides authorship; CPU authors intent, GPU
realizes; the body never learns who drove it. THE PLAYER IS A DRIVER,
NOT A PROTAGONIST — the controlled pawn is the biped body wearing the
input cast. Evidence already in the tree: the ribbon's one-integrator
law; K5 possession; slot 0; corral. Derivable when pulled, one seam
each: controllable floaters; algorithmic pawns; driver swaps as
musical events.
THE FOUR STRATA (the arrow law's semantics):
S1 THE SURFACE — the reference frame everything is measured against.
S2 THE SURFACE'S LIFECYCLE — the frame itself is created and evicted
   (patches, tiles, streaming): the world-engine as a stratum.
S3 PLACEMENT — bodies enter the frame (spawn, footprints,
   negotiation).
S4 MOTION — drivers move placed bodies within the bodies' own
   constraints.
Each stratum depends only downward. The spine's confession decomposes
along exactly these lines.

## 10 — THE RELATIONAL ANATOMY (v2; the four abstractions)
An entity's behavior decomposes into four relations:
PLACEMENT — how it enters and where it may sit (lifecycle's spatial
face; the Placeable core).
TERRAIN-RELATION — its permanent contract with the surface: ONE HEART,
many casts. Sample the surface, apply an offset, oscillate: the pawn
is offset-zero with normal alignment (snap); the ribbon is
offset-plus-clearance with a floor; the floaters are the same
relation wiggling differently. BOUND and REFERENCED are casts of one
function family. REALIZATION GUARD: the abstraction unifies now; the
implementation stays specialized where the substrate demands
(pawn_ground_resolve lives under the FXC no-new-branches law) —
unification of realization is pull-based and bus-side-aware, never a
cleanup crusade.
PEER-RELATION — footprints, separation, awareness, the signal: the
occupier face and the doors law govern it.
GAIT — the motion texture, a cast on the body, commanded by drivers
(§9) and constrained by the terrain-relation. Gait is design; the
driver is authorship; the terrain-relation is law.
The four relations are where species differ and where simplification
lives: two entities that share a relation should share its cast.
