## 0 — THE INTENTION (carried, completed)
An API understood immediately: logical, compositional, no foggy
reasoning. Fog is influence traveling undeclared paths; entanglement
is knowledge traveling undeclared paths; the theory forbids both
structurally. The payoff is GENERATIVITY: novelty is recombination.
And the reason the API's quality is the first priority: THE API IS
THE BRIDGE BETWEEN THE AUTHOR AND HIS COLLABORATORS — human or
otherwise. Synchronicity of mental models is the project's real
infrastructure; the code is its written form.

## 1 — ROLES, CASTS, AND THE GRAMMAR LAW
Every noun is a ROLE (declared interface with laws) or a CAST (an
implementation currently playing it). COMPOSITION IS CASTING.
Swapping a cast never edits another cast — only the role's contract
binds them.
THE GRAMMAR LAW (v3): THE TEMPLATE IS THE RELATION GRAMMAR; A DEMO IS
A SENTENCE IN IT. The template fixes relations — strata, roles,
doors, rest = identity, the cascade, GPU sovereignty; it has no look.
A demo fixes casts; demos have looks. A demo may never edit a
relation: if one needs to, the template is missing a role, and that
is a theory amendment, not a demo option. Practice companion: THE
DEMO CONTRACT (five axes; the flagship pair; the staged program),
ratified with this document.

## 2 — THE LAYERS AND THE CENTRAL LAW (carried)
L0 SUBSTRATE · L1 PRIMITIVES · L2 ENTITIES · L3 COUPLINGS · L4
ORCHESTRATION · L5 REALIZATION. Every behavior is AUTONOMOUS or
COUPLED; no third kind. Coupled = a named channel, one author, a
REST reproducing the autonomous self exactly, a composition law, the
categorical boundary (receivers get values, never sources).
COMPOSITION LAWS COME IN TWO KINDS: scalar deviations compose
ADDITIVELY over inviolate idleness; intent authorship composes by
ARBITRATION (possession is the precedent). The generalized silence
test holds in both. Entities share functions and vocabulary freely,
NEVER state.

## 3 — THE SUBSTRATE (carried, grounded)
MAP, TERRITORY, REFERENCE FRAMES, CONVERGENCE, GROUNDING — that is
the terrain: the stage all interaction is measured against, the one
mandatory relation, the constrainer of spatial distribution and
movement. (The program's two halves share this vocabulary by design:
the analyzer's territory grounds pitch as the terrain grounds
bodies.) K1 HAS MODES: bound / referenced / anchored-elsewhere. K1 IS
BIDIRECTIONAL: entities may write the ground; the Surface carries the
authoring channel. THE LOCAL-FRAME LAW: entities speak frame_at,
never world axes; the geodesic price is exactly this discipline.

## 4 — THE OCCUPIER, THE COVERING LAW, AND THE PIER SPLIT
OCCUPIER: a thing that claims a footprint and teaches the terrain to
cover it. THE COVERING LAW holds. THE PIER SPLIT (v3): the historical
pier fused two concepts the taxonomy always kept apart —
  COLLISION IS A RULE: the Occupier QUERY FACE (inside_at /
  edge_distance_at / height_at) over machinery that already exists;
  entitlement to it is declared per species (T3).
  THE PLINTH IS AN AESTHETIC: a stamp (T4) an entity MAY make because
  it looks good; demo-optional, never load-bearing for correctness.
Piers are thereby demoted from system to CAST: one realization of
rule + ornament, kept where it pleases the eye, replaceable where it
does not. The FXC realization laws (proxied rect solids, bounded
loops, no new branches) are CAST-SCOPED and stand untouched. The
honest boundary holds: a heightfield is one-height-per-column; the
substrate-direction generalization remains a declared future cast.

## 8 — THE ARROW LAW (carried, plus grades)
Dependencies point root-to-leaf, never back. Modules include only
contracts, services, primitives; peers know vocabulary, never state;
boundary-crossing DTOs live in the boundary's contract; a module
exists per owned decision. THE DELETION TEST, two grades: ROOT-ONLY
(end-state) and FACES-ONLY (transitional pass: every non-root edit
falls on a declared face).

## 9 — THE DRIVER LAW, THREE ACTS, AND THE FOUR STRATA
ACT I — A MOVING THING IS A BODY TIMES A DRIVER. The body owns K1
mode, constraints, the integrator, the realization; drivers (input /
algorithm / music) write intents through it; arbitration decides
authorship; the body never learns who drove it. The player is a
driver, not a protagonist.
ACT II — INTENTS HAVE ADDRESSES (v3): one body, a species, a spatial
set, or the trait-class MOVERS itself. Each addressed body TRANSLATES
the verb through its own gait under its own constraints. What moves
may move individually or collectively; F6's gather and a held chord
gathering the world are one sentence with different authors.
Direction currently smeared into species (corral, kite, hardwired
key routing) migrates to the direction layer (§13) as pulled.
ACT III — THE ANCHOR (v3): what we control, in the end, is the
CAMERA; the controlled body is merely its current ANCHOR. Anchor is
a role; pawn, possessed agent, the rider's seat, the photographer
are its casts; possession is re-anchoring.
THE FOUR STRATA (carried): S1 the surface · S2 the surface's
lifecycle · S3 placement · S4 motion — each depending only downward.
The complete motion statement: everything moves WITH the frame (D2,
the surface's voice); some things also move IN it (drivers); what
moves in it can be addressed one at a time or all at once (Act II).

## 11 — THE WITNESS SYSTEM: CAMERA, ANCHOR, BUBBLE (v3)
THE CAMERA is a special entity — the player's eyes, mouse-owned
always, coupled to the anchor, part of the theatrical illusion: no
footprint, no collision, its own small contract (anchoring modes,
the level-horizon law it already carries).
THE ANCHOR (§9 Act III) is what the camera couples to.
THE BUBBLE: around the anchor lives a bounded radius of awareness —
and the bound is the theory, not an obstacle: AWARENESS IS A SCARCE,
BUDGETED RESOURCE (N slots, hardware-shaped; the 64-solid resolve
loop and the aura grid are its two existing casts). COLLISION
ENTITLEMENT (T3) means buying slots in the budget; playability is
the art of spending it. Declared underdeveloped, direction M-q; its
destiny is doubled: the interaction system, AND an EVENT SOURCE —
entries, exits, counts, nearest-distance — a coming family of
proximity READINGS for couplings. Proximity becomes musical
material; the aura was the prototype all along.

## 12 — THE SEEDED SAMPLER (v3; one tool, many tables)
The program distributes design and spawn probability one way,
written many times. The tool underneath is single: the SEEDED DESIGN
SAMPLER — (seed, design-space table) -> individual — with the bucket

inputs, property indices, and draw order are frozen vocabulary; the
machine unifies, the biography never shifts.

## 13 — THE DIRECTION LAYER (v3; being vs direction)
THE BODY OWNS HOW; DIRECTION OWNS WHEN AND WHOM. Choreography —
scene verbs, collective addresses, staged sequences — is
orchestration, homed at the cartridge or the direction module it
grows (M-p), speaking Act II's addressed intents. Entity files carry
being: laws, gaits, translations of verbs. Corral, kite, and
hardwired scene keys are direction currently living inside species;
they migrate as pulled. Direction is also where demos choreograph:
a demo's scene-writing is sentences in this layer, never edits to
bodies.

## 14 — THE DEMO GRAMMAR (v3; the contract's summary)
Five axes a demo varies: D1 the surface cast (extent;
streamed-procedural or generated-once) · D2 the surface's voice
(geometry and color pipes; the wave rewired) · D3 the population
(roster + design tables + future profiles) · D4 the skins · D5 the
composition (coupling set, seed, mood profile). The flagship pair —
the gallery exhibit and the musician template — is the completion
test: one grammar, both sentences, a stranger composing a third
without editing a relation.

## 15 — THE ENGINE AS CAST (v3; portability)
THE PROGRAM IS THE THEORY; WebGPU IS L5'S CURRENT CAST. GPU
sovereignty split intent from realization; the arrow law pushed
realization to the leaves; strata, roles, drivers, sampler, witness,
contracts are engine-agnostic sentences. Leaving Dawn would be a
recast of L5 plus retirement of the cast-scoped FXC laws — everything
above that line travels whole. The systematicity is the asset; the
engine is its current instrument.
