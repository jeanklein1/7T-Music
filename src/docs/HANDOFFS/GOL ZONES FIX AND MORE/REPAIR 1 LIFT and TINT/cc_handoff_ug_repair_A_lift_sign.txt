WHY
---
pawn_gol_suppression returns the suppression FACTOR: 1 at the pawn,
0 beyond ZONE_SUPPRESS_OUTER (15 wu). Its own banner says so, and the
three walker-family consumers all multiply the GoL lift by its
COMPLEMENT:

    gol * (1.0 - supp_factor)          query_ground_walker
    gol * (1.0 - supp_factor)          query_ground_walker_tilt
    gol * (1.0 - supp_factor)          query_ground_walker_pair

The two UNIFIED_GROUND_1 render sites multiply by the factor itself.
The consequence is total and exact:

    d >= 15 wu from pawn   factor 0   lift 0     cap sits on the base
                                                 surface, curtain quads
                                                 degenerate -> NO GoL
                                                 anywhere in the world
    4 -> 15 wu             ramp       ramp       an inverted bow wave
    d <= 4 wu              factor 1   full       the cell under the
                                                 pawn's feet EXTRUDES

Reported symptom, verbatim: cells and extrusions gone from the map, and
rarely one extrudes when the pawn passes by. "Rarely" is the spawn
arithmetic, not a second bug: SPAWN_CHANCE 0.15 x HEIGHT_CHANCE 0.30 x
force_no_height tier rows x the U5 vocabulary mask, and then the pawn
must physically walk onto one.

The repair restores the ratified UNIFIED_GROUND_1 semantics: GoL
de-extrudes under the pawn so he passes smoothly, and render agrees
with query_ground_walker_pair, which already de-extrudes correctly.
