WHY
---
The tint pre-filters candidate zones by lattice-node equality:

    floor(zp.origin / MODE_LATTICE_SPACING) == floor(world_xz / 120)

That assumes a zone never leaves its own 120 wu node. At 32 cells a zone
is already 100 wu; above ~38 cells it cannot hold, and fragments across
the node boundary stop finding their zone entirely. The bounds test that
follows the pre-filter IS the membership test — the pre-filter adds
nothing but the assumption.

Its out-of-bounds exit is also `break`, which abandons the remaining
slots instead of trying the next zone.

THIS COMMIT IS A PROVABLE NO-OP TODAY. Every authored tier is 16, 24 or
32 cells = 50, 75 or 100 wu, centred on a node at (n+0.5)*120 with at
least 10 wu of margin per side; the corner snap moves a zone by at most
one cell (3.125). No zone in the current tables can straddle a node
boundary, and no node bears two zones (the CPU's `exists` check). So
neither the pre-filter nor the break/continue distinction can fire.

That is the point. The gate for this commit is that NOTHING CHANGES.
What it buys is S3: this is the FS's last dependency on
MODE_LATTICE_SPACING, and removing it is what lets a zone exceed 120 wu.
