WHY
---
A curtain quad welds a cap perimeter vertex to its base twin at the SAME
world XZ, so the wall is a vertical face standing exactly ON the cell
boundary. Every fragment on it has world_pos.x = (N+1) * 3.125, and
cell_address floors that to N+1: the neighbour. 3.125 is 25/8 and exact
in f32, so this is deterministic, not jitter — the east and north walls
of every extruded checker render solidly in the neighbour's colour.

This is invisible today only because those walls are DEGENERATE before
handoff A: the cap vertex at lx = 4 already sits at the neighbour's
height, so the quad has zero drop and never rasterizes. Handoff A gives
them full height. A alone therefore makes this symptom appear. A and B
must land together.

The INCIDENT #3 clamp does not help — it re-homes patch-border slivers
only, and mid-patch the neighbour's texel is in-domain.

The remedy is the charter's own: ONE-ADDRESS LAW (C8). The VS knows the
owning cell exactly. Making the FS re-derive it from an interpolated
position that is deliberately ambiguous is the same second addressing
INCIDENT #3 already convicted for patch_uv. Carry it.

Legacy and skirt fragments keep the world floor plus the clamp. Their
band is the LOD1/soft space, the existing path is correct there, and a
legacy quad's four vertices do NOT share one cell — so a flat varying
would depend on the provoking vertex. The .z flag makes the two
topologies explicit instead of relying on index-buffer winding.

UG_REPAIR_2 B - the FS carries the owning cell, it does not re-derive it

A curtain face stands exactly on the cell boundary, so every fragment on
it has world_pos.x = (N+1) * 3.125 and cell_address floors to N+1. The
east and north walls of an extruded checker therefore read the
neighbour's colour. 3.125 is exact in f32, so this is deterministic. The

flag: cap and base fragments take the carried address, legacy and skirt
keep the world floor and the clamp. This is the ONE-ADDRESS LAW applied
to the new topology - the same second addressing INCIDENT #3 convicted
for patch_uv. The chimera is now inexpressible by construction rather
than repaired by clamp.
