WHY — this is no longer housekeeping
--------------------------------------------------------------------
A0 §5f corrected the plan. The stale footprint does not displace a zone:
ZONE_EXTENT/2 = 50 = 16 x 3.125 is an exact multiple of the snap grid,
so the CPU's centre already coincides with the GPU's origin. Verified
for grids 8/16/24/32/64.

The defect is the RADIUS, and it runs both ways:

  - at 16 cells (50 wu) a fixed r=50 over-reserves sixteenfold in area,
    so the spawn engine rejects neighbours it should accept;
  - at 64 cells (200 wu) it UNDER-reserves. The inscribed circle would
    be 100. With r=50, two zones on adjacent 120 wu lattice nodes can
    overlap by up to 80 wu.

The second is the dangerous one and the plan did not anticipate it.

Overlap is not a cosmetic problem. contrib_gol_zones_at RETURNS on its
first covering zone; the tint BREAKS on its first. Neither defines an
order, and they do not even filter alike — contrib skips zones with
alive_height < 0.01 before matching, the tint does not. Two overlapping
zones can therefore give a cell its height from one and its colour from
the other. That chimera is currently unreachable only because no zone
is large enough to overlap. S3 makes it reachable.

The footprint registry already exists to prevent exactly this
(check_position / register_footprint, PopFamily::GOL). It is not
broken - it is being told a lie. Tell it the truth and the overlap
question closes without a new mechanism, and first-match-wins becomes
deterministic because at most one zone can cover any cell.

Use the CIRCUMSCRIBED radius, not the inscribed one: 0.5 * hypot(w, h).
Conservative in the safe direction. It over-reserves for thin-and-long
zones, which is a density cost Jean has been told about and accepted as
the first move; rectangles in the registry are a later question.

COMMIT MESSAGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
UG_FIELDS_1 S2 - the GoL footprint tells the truth

ZONE_EXTENT and FOOTPRINT_RADIUS survived U5, which made a zone's extent
tier-derived. The centre was never wrong - 50 wu is an exact multiple of
the 3.125 snap, so the CPU's centre already coincided with the GPU's
origin. The radius was.

A fixed inscribed circle of a 100 wu square over-reserves sixteenfold in
area at 16 cells and UNDER-reserves at 64, where two zones on adjacent
120 wu lattice nodes could overlap by 80. Overlap is not cosmetic:
contrib_gol_zones_at returns on its first covering zone and the tint
breaks on its first, with no defined order and unequal filters, so
overlapping zones could give one cell its height from one zone and its
colour from another. Unreachable today; reachable the moment S3 lands.

Both constants retire. The footprint is now the circumscribed radius of
the tier's own rectangle - conservative, so the registry can never
permit an overlap, and first-match-wins becomes deterministic because at
most one zone covers any cell. The corner snap uses the tier's extent
per axis instead of a fixed 100.
