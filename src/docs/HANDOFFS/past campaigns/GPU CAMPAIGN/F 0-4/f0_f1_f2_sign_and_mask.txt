visible anywhere; terrain extruding upward at a spot only when the
pawn passes; a major performance hit. Design-side investigation found
TWO AUTHORING ERRORS IN THE HANDOFF SPECS (not in CC's execution) and
one under-priced structural cost:
  (1) U3's spec multiplied the render lift by the suppression FACTOR
      instead of its COMPLEMENT — the lift is inverted: zero
      everywhere except within 15 wu of the pawn.
  (2) U5's mask landed UNIVERSAL; ~83% of the world is smooth ground,
      so most zones are fully masked at birth and can never lift.

      per-frame VS work (the real resource).

0 × (1−supp). Rest-identity gates cannot validate a multiplicative
factor whose multiplicand is zero at rest. Noted for future gate
design: a motion gate with a known-truth reference is required.
